1. Explore by Category

Edges & Interiors publishes independent, in-depth writing on the built environment. Our categories reflect the subjects we return to most often — the intersections of design, material, light, and space. Each category gathers essays, case studies, interviews, and visual stories.

Plain-language summary: Every article is filed under one or more categories. Use these pages to find the themes that matter to you. Our editorial team curates each category with care.

3. How We Organise Content

Every article on Edges & Interiors is assigned at least one primary category. Longer features may also appear in secondary categories where relevant. Our editorial team reviews category assignments regularly to ensure they remain useful and accurate.

If you are looking for a specific topic that isn’t listed here, try the search function or contact us — we welcome suggestions for new categories.

3.1 Editorial independence

Categories are not sponsored. We do not accept payment to place content in a particular category, and our editorial taxonomy is managed independently of any commercial relationship.

3.2 Contributing

We welcome pitches and submissions. If you have a story that fits one of our categories, please review our contact page for submission guidelines. We do not charge for editorial consideration.
