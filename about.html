About the Publication

Writing the built environment with depth & intention.

Edges & Interiors is an independent journal of interior design, architecture, and material culture—founded on the belief that thoughtful writing can elevate the way we inhabit space.

We publish long-form essays, practical guides, and critical perspectives for designers, architects, and discerning homeowners. No trends. No sponsored fluff. Just honest, researched, and beautifully considered journalism.

Our Manifesto

Depth over speed

We take time to research, interview, and refine. Every article is written to be read slowly—and to remain useful years from now.

No sponsored content

Our editorial is entirely independent. We do not accept payment for coverage, and we clearly label any partnerships or advertisements.

Material honesty

We celebrate materials that reveal their origins—raw wood, stone, metal, concrete—and the craftspeople who shape them.

Design as generosity

Good design serves people and planet. We champion spaces that are sustainable, humane, and quietly beautiful.

Critical, not cynical

We ask hard questions about the built environment, but always with a constructive spirit and a love for the discipline.

Global, local

Our perspective is international, but we pay close attention to regional materials, climate, and craft traditions.

The Editorial Team

Elena Marchetti

Editor-in-Chief

Elena is an architectural writer and former interior architect. She founded Edges & Interiors in 2018 to create a space for slow, considered writing about the built environment.

James Okafor

Senior Editor, Architecture

James is a registered architect and critic. He leads our architecture coverage, with a particular interest in adaptive reuse and public space.

Sofia Lindqvist

Editor, Materials & Interiors

Sofia is a materials specialist and interior designer. She writes about sustainable finishes, craft, and the sensory experience of space.

What guides us

01

Rigour. Every claim is researched, every project visited, every quote verified. We fact-check obsessively.

02

Clarity. We write in plain language, avoiding jargon unless it serves the reader. Good design writing should be accessible.

03

Independence. Our only allegiance is to our readers. We are not beholden to advertisers, brands, or trends.

04

Beauty. We believe in the power of well-composed images and typography to enhance understanding and pleasure.
