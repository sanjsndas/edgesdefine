    • Cookies Policy

    We use cookies to make your experience on Edges & Interiors smoother, to understand how you interact with our content, and to keep our site secure. This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can control them.

    Last updated: 1 June 2025

    In short: We use essential cookies to run our site, analytics cookies to understand what’s read, and preference cookies to remember your choices. You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Disabling some cookies may affect how the site works.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to provide information to site owners, and to remember your preferences. Cookies do not typically contain any information that personally identifies you, but personal data we store about you may be linked to information obtained from cookies.

    2. How We Use Cookies

    Edges & Interiors uses cookies for the following purposes:

    • Essential / strictly necessary cookies: required for the website to function, such as navigating pages, accessing secure areas, and enabling basic features.
    • Analytics and performance cookies: help us understand how visitors use our site — which pages are most visited, how long people stay, and if they encounter errors. This data is aggregated and anonymous.
    • Functionality and preference cookies: remember choices you make (e.g., language, region, or display preferences) to provide a more personalised experience.
    • Marketing and advertising cookies (if applicable): used to deliver relevant advertisements and track the effectiveness of our campaigns. We do not currently serve third-party ads, but we may use limited marketing cookies for our own promotions.

    We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information, and we never sell cookie data to third parties.

    3. Types of Cookies We Use

    Below is a detailed list of the specific cookies that may be set when you visit our website. Please note that third-party cookies (e.g., from analytics providers) are governed by their own privacy policies.

    Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
    ei_session Essential Preserves user session state across page requests. Session
    ei_preferences Functionality Stores your display and layout preferences (e.g., font size, theme). 1 year
    _ga Analytics (Google) Used to distinguish users and generate statistical data on site usage. 2 years
    _gid Analytics (Google) Used to distinguish users for analytics reporting. 24 hours
    _gat Analytics (Google) Used to throttle request rate to Google Analytics. 1 minute
    cookie_consent Essential Records whether you have accepted the cookie notice. 1 year

    We may also use similar technologies such as web beacons (clear GIFs) and local storage to analyse trends and administer the site. These are covered by this policy.

    4. Third-Party Cookies

    Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. For example, we use Google Analytics to understand site traffic. Google may use cookies to collect information about your interactions with our site, which helps us improve our content. We do not control these third-party cookies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these providers:

    • Google Privacy Policy
    • How Google uses cookies

    5. Your Cookie Choices

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences in several ways:

    • Browser settings: most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these in the “Options” or “Preferences” menu. You can also delete existing cookies from your browser.
    • Third-party opt-outs: you can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
    • Our consent notice: when you first visit our site, you may see a banner asking for your consent to non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site.

    Please note that blocking or disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website and limit your experience.

    6. Do Not Track Signals

    Some browsers have a “Do Not Track” (DNT) feature that lets you tell websites you do not want to be tracked. Our website does not currently respond to DNT signals, as there is no industry consensus on how to interpret them. We will continue to monitor developments and may adopt a standard in the future.

    7. Changes to This Cookies Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.
